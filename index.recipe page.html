Chocolate Chip Cookies

A simple and delicious classic recipe for soft, chewy chocolate chip cookies that everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix butter and sugar together until smooth.
  3. Stir in flour and chocolate chips until a dough forms.
  4. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until golden brown.
